Questions

How do I inquire about an artwork?

Use the form on this page, or call or email us. Pick the artwork in the "About" list so we know which piece you mean.

Who made each artwork?

Every artwork shows the name of its artist. Open the Artists tab to see their works and visit their Facebook page.

How can I see an artwork bigger?

Click the artwork in the Gallery tab. A larger view opens with the full story. Use the arrow buttons or the keyboard arrows to move between artworks.

How do I save my favorites?

Tap the heart on an artwork card. Then use the Favorites button in the Gallery tab to see only your saved artworks. Your favorites stay in this browser.
